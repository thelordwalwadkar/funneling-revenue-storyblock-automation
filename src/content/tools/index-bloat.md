faq:
  - q: "What is index bloat?"
    a: "Index bloat is when a site has many low-value URLs in Google's index, such as filtered or sorted versions of a page, duplicates and thin archives. They dilute crawl effort and make the strong pages harder to find."
  - q: "How can the tool see my index if Search Console does not list it?"
    a: "It estimates bloat from the URLs Google shows in search results, which are the ones that matter. For the biggest flagged URLs you can ask Google directly whether each one is indexed."
  - q: "Should I noindex every flagged URL?"
    a: "No. Parameter and duplicate URLs usually need a canonical tag or redirect, archives often need noindex, and thin pages may need better content instead. Each flag explains its own fix."

    a: "Yes. Filters on product lists, sorting options and download or spec pages can multiply URLs quickly, and the ones that earn no clicks add up."
