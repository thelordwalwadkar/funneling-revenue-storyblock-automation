  - >-
    If a metric doesn't tie to pipeline, it doesn't belong on the dashboard. That sentence sounds obvious until you look at what most manufacturing marketing reports actually contain: impressions, sessions, bounce rate, domain authority, keyword count, follower growth. Numbers that make a slide look good and tell a CFO nothing.
  - >-
    We don't report those numbers, and we think most agencies keep reporting them because the alternative is harder — measuring pipeline value, deal velocity, and revenue attribution requires actually tying marketing activity to the CRM, not just the analytics dashboard. It's more work. It's also the only version of the report that survives contact with a board meeting.
  - >-
    This pillar exists to make that case in detail: which metrics matter for a nine-month industrial sales cycle, how to build attribution when the buying committee has five people on it, and why the vanity metrics that agencies lead with are usually the ones easiest to inflate.
