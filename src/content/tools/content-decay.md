faq:
  - q: "What is content decay?"
    a: "Content decay is the slow loss of search traffic on a page that used to perform. It usually happens because the information has aged, competitors have published something newer, or the page no longer matches what people search for."
  - q: "How does the tool tell a real decline from a seasonal dip?"
    a: "It needs at least 13 months of history. When a page is down against its peak, the latest month is compared with the same month a year earlier. If it is close to last year, the dip is treated as seasonal; if it is well below, it is a real decline."
  - q: "Is it safe to connect my Search Console?"

  - q: "What should I do with a page marked content refresh?"
    a: "Impressions are down as well as clicks, so Google is showing the page less. Update the facts, add what competitors now cover, improve the title and headings, and link to it from newer pages."
