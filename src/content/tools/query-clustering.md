faq:
  - q: "What is query clustering?"
    a: "It is grouping search queries that carry the same intent into topics, so content is planned and measured per topic instead of per keyword."
  - q: "How does the tool decide two queries belong together?"
    a: "They share the same words in any order, or one of your pages earns a real share of both and they have enough words in common. The shared page is a proxy for Google treating them as one question."
  - q: "What does split across pages mean?"
    a: "Two or more of your pages each hold a meaningful share of the topic's impressions. Decide which page should own the topic, then merge or re-target the others."
  - q: "How is this different from the cannibalization checker?"
    a: "Cannibalization looks at one query at a time and how your pages compete for it. Clustering looks at a whole topic made of many related queries, which is how you would plan content."
