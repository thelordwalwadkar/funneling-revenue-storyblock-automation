  - title: "We keep only meaningful drops"
    body: "A query is shown only when its clicks fell by at least a floor based on the typical query, so normal noise on small queries stays out."

faq:
  - q: "Why did my queries lose clicks?"
    a: "The usual causes are a lost or weaker ranking, less search demand, or a worse click-through rate because a competitor or a new result type pushed you down the page. The tool labels which one applies to each query."
  - q: "How is a demand drop different from a ranking drop?"
    a: "In a demand drop your position held but fewer people searched, so there is little to fix on the page. In a ranking drop your position slipped, so the page or its competitors changed."
  - q: "What is a lost query?"
    a: "A query that earned clicks in the earlier period and almost none in the recent one. Check whether the page was changed, redirected, removed or de-indexed."
  - q: "Why does the tool ask for brand terms?"
    a: "Brand swings are usually caused by marketing activity, not SEO, so they are not the losses you can act on. Excluding them keeps the list useful."
