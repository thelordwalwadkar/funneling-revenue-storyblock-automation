faq:
  - q: "What is keyword cannibalization?"
    a: "It is when two or more pages on your site target or rank for the same search query, so they split clicks and signals between them instead of one page ranking strongly."
  - q: "Is cannibalization always a problem?"
    a: "No. If one page takes most of the clicks, it is a clear owner and you can leave it. It becomes a problem when the ranking page keeps changing, or the pages sit close together without a winner."
  - q: "Why does the tool ask me to exclude brand terms?"
    a: "A brand query where one page rightly dominates, such as your homepage for your company name, is not cannibalization. Excluding it keeps the list focused on queries you can act on."
  - q: "How should I fix a High-priority conflict?"
    a: "Choose one page to own the query. Merge the weaker page into it or redirect it, or re-target it at a different question. Then point internal links and anchor text at the page you chose."
