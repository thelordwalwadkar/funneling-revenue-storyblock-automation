    a: "A keyword is in striking distance when your page already ranks just outside the top results, usually positions 4 to 20. Small improvements can move it up and bring a large share of extra clicks."
  - q: "Why are these called quick wins?"
    a: "The page already has some relevance and visibility. Improving the title, adding depth, and linking to it from related pages is usually faster than ranking a brand-new page from nothing."
  - q: "How accurate is the click estimate?"
    a: "It is an estimate, not a forecast. It applies typical click-through rates to the impressions you already get, so it is best used to rank opportunities against each other."
