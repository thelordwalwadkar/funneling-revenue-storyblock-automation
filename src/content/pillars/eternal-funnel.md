  - >-
    The funnel describes how people make decisions. It is not a marketing framework, which is why it has survived every medium. Newspapers gave way to radio, radio to television, television to search, search to social, and now social is giving way to AI. Every generation of marketers has declared the funnel dead and reinvented it under a new name. It never was dead. Only the channel changed.
  - >-
    For manufacturers, this matters more than it does for consumer brands, because the sales cycle is long enough to watch the whole sequence play out in slow motion. A procurement engineer moves from awareness to consideration to decision over months, sometimes over a year, across a dozen touchpoints and several stakeholders. If you can't see which stage is leaking, you can't fix your pipeline — you can only guess.
  - >-
    This pillar is where we make the case for the funnel as a diagnostic tool, not a relic. Every post here should help you answer one question: which stage of my funnel is actually broken, and what does the evidence say I should do about it?
