howItWorks:
  - title: "We compare recent months with the months before"
    body: "You sign in with Google and pick a comparison such as 3 months against the 3 before. Only completed calendar months are used, so a partial month never skews the result."
  - title: "We keep only meaningful gains"
    body: "A query is shown only when its clicks rose by at least a floor based on the typical query, so one extra click on a tiny query does not make the list."
  - title: "We work out why each query grew"
    body: "Position, impressions and clicks are compared between the two periods to label each gain as new, ranking, demand or click-rate driven."
faq:
  - q: "What counts as a rising keyword?"
    a: "A query whose clicks in the recent period are higher than in the earlier period by more than a significance floor. The floor is the larger of a few clicks and a share of the typical query's clicks."
  - q: "What is the difference between ranking growth and demand growth?"
    a: "Ranking growth means your average position improved. Demand growth means position held but more people searched, so impressions rose. They need different follow-up."
  - q: "What is an internal shift?"
    a: "If a query gained clicks on one page while another of your pages lost nearly the same amount, the traffic only moved around. The tool sets these aside instead of counting them as growth."
  - q: "How do I use the results?"
    a: "Find the pattern behind your wins: topics, page types or formats that are working. Then add depth to those pages and build related content around them."
